# RUH

RUH is a single-weight serif display typeface developed for RŪḤ, a fragrance atelier in New Orleans. Its high-contrast strokes, open counters and restrained contours are intended for short headlines and venue announcements. It includes the macron U and dot-below H required by the brand name, common accented Latin characters, currency symbols and tabular numeral alternatives.

The project follows original Midjourney lettering studies and GPT Image glyph studies under Tariq Shaheed Johnson's creative direction. The glyphs were traced, normalized and engineered into OpenType fonts with explicit spacing, kerning and mark positioning. Source-generation and engineering records are disclosed; the project does not present the letterforms as entirely hand-drawn.

RUH is a display face. A complementary text face is appropriate for dense copy and small interface information. One Regular style is currently developed.

The font software is released under OFL 1.1. Google Fonts naming-exception review, human submission attestations and catalog acceptance remain pending.
