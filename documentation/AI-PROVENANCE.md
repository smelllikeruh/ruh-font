# Production provenance

Tariq Shaheed Johnson supplied the RŪḤ project direction, selected a sleek display treatment and commissioned the typeface. Midjourney supplied initial headline direction. GPT Image produced coordinated uppercase, lowercase, numeral/symbol and extended Latin raster studies, including a corrected lowercase-l study.

The selected letterforms were segmented, traced, normalized and assembled using a Codex-assisted FontTools/UFO workflow. Supporting accents, punctuation, tabular numeral alternatives, combining-mark behavior and corrected forms include explicit vector construction and engineering. The public source is the final editable UFO and the deterministic build code.

The project does not claim that the forms are entirely hand-drawn, that every encoded character was individually image-generated, or that an image service directly produced an installable font. AI tools are disclosed as tools, not human signatories. The raw generation sheets and exact generation records are retained in the owner's separate complete Font Pack.

The owner authorized release of the font family and editable font source under OFL 1.1 on 6 September 2026. Google's separate contributor agreement and copyright-authority submission attestation still require the appropriate human's review; this document does not make or sign those declarations.
